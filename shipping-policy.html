📋 Lost or Damaged Packages

We take full responsibility for ensuring your order arrives safely and in perfect condition:

Lost Package Policy

  • If tracking shows no movement for 7+ days, contact us immediately
  • We'll initiate a search with the shipping carrier within 24 hours
  • If not found within 5 business days, we'll reship or refund
  • Lost package claims must be filed within 30 days of shipment

Damage Policy

  • Inspect your package upon delivery for any damage
  • Take photos of damaged items and packaging
  • Report damage within 48 hours of delivery
  • We'll arrange replacement or full refund, including return shipping

Important Instructions

Do not refuse damaged packages. Accept the delivery, document the damage, and contact us immediately for resolution.

Insurance Coverage

  • All shipments include basic insurance up to ₹5,000
  • Additional insurance available for high-value items
  • Signature required for orders above ₹2,000
  • Secure packaging to prevent damage during transit
