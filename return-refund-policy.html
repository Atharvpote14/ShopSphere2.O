Return & Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Learn about our hassle-free return and refund process.

📦 Return Eligibility

Most items purchased from ShopSphere are eligible for return within the specified timeframe. To be eligible for a return, your item must meet the following criteria:

Timeframe

Within 30 days of delivery date

Condition

Unused, unworn, and in original condition

Packaging

Original packaging, tags, and labels intact

Proof of Purchase

Order confirmation or receipt required

Important Note

Items must be returned in the same condition as received, with all original accessories and documentation included.

⏰ Return Timeframes

ShopSphere offers different return periods based on product categories:

Product Category Return Period Conditions
Clothing & Accessories 30 days Tags attached, unworn
Electronics 15 days Sealed packaging, all accessories
Home & Living 30 days Original packaging, undamaged
Books & Media 7 days Unopened, sealed condition
Sports & Outdoors 30 days Unused, original condition

Return Period Calculation

The return period starts from the date of delivery, not the purchase date. Check your order confirmation for exact delivery dates.

🚫 Non-Returnable Items

Certain items cannot be returned due to hygiene, safety, or customization reasons:

Personal Care

Underwear, swimwear, cosmetics, and personal hygiene products

Custom Items

Personalized products, custom-made items, and engraved products

Perishables

Food items, flowers, and other perishable goods

Digital Products

Software, digital downloads, and gift cards

Final Sale Items

Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged unless defective or damaged upon arrival.

🔄 Exchange Policy

ShopSphere offers easy exchanges for different sizes, colors, or styles of the same product:

Exchange Process

  • Exchanges must be requested within the return period
  • Item must meet all return eligibility criteria
  • Price differences will be charged or refunded accordingly
  • Exchange shipping is free for the first exchange

Exchange Benefits

Exchanges are processed faster than refunds and you'll receive your new item within 5-7 business days after we receive your return.

Exchange Limitations

  • Only one exchange per original order
  • Exchanges are subject to product availability
  • Custom and personalized items cannot be exchanged

💰 Refund Processing

Refunds are processed promptly once we receive and inspect your returned item:

Return Received

We'll send you an email confirmation when your return is received at our facility.

Quality Inspection

Our team inspects the returned item within 1-2 business days to ensure it meets return criteria.

Refund Initiated

Once approved, your refund is processed and sent back to your original payment method.

Refund Completed

Refunds typically appear in your account within 5-10 business days, depending on your payment provider.

You can cancel your order before it ships for a full refund:

Cancellation Timeframes

How to Cancel

Go to "My Orders" in your account, select the order, and click "Cancel Order". You'll receive a confirmation email within minutes.

📋 How to Initiate a Return

Starting a return is simple and can be done through multiple channels:

Log into Your Account

Go to "My Orders" and select the order you want to return.

Select Items to Return

Choose the items you want to return and specify the reason for return.

Print Return Label

Download and print the prepaid return shipping label from your account.

Package and Ship

Pack the item securely with all original packaging and drop it off at any authorized shipping location.

Return Shipping

ShopSphere provides prepaid return labels for most returns. For international orders, additional shipping fees may apply.

🛡️ Return Shipping & Insurance

We take care of return shipping to make the process hassle-free for our customers:

Shipping Coverage

  • Free return shipping for all domestic orders
  • Return shipping insurance included up to ₹10,000
  • Multiple return locations available for your convenience
  • Real-time return tracking through your account

Package Your Return Safely

  • Use the original packaging when possible
  • Include all accessories, manuals, and documentation
  • Remove any old shipping labels
  • Secure the package to prevent damage during transit

Lost Returns

If your return is lost in transit, contact us immediately with your tracking number. We'll work with the shipping carrier to locate your package or process a refund.
