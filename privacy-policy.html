Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how ShopSphere collects, uses, and protects your information.

📋 Information We Collect

At ShopSphere, we collect various types of information to provide you with the best shopping experience:

Personal Information

Name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing addresses

Account Information

Username, password (encrypted), purchase history, preferences

Payment Information

Credit/debit card details, billing information (processed securely)

Technical Information

IP address, browser type, device information, browsing behavior

How We Collect Information

  • When you create an account or make a purchase
  • When you browse our website and use our services
  • When you communicate with our customer support
  • Through cookies and similar technologies
  • When you participate in promotions or surveys

🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use your information for various purposes to enhance your shopping experience:

Primary Uses

Processing orders, managing your account, providing customer support, personalizing your shopping experience, and sending important notifications about your orders.

Specific Purposes

  • Order Processing: To process, fulfill, and deliver your orders
  • Account Management: To create and manage your ShopSphere account
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide assistance
  • Personalization: To recommend products and customize your experience
  • Marketing: To send promotional offers (with your consent)
  • Security: To detect fraud and maintain platform security
  • Analytics: To improve our services and user experience

🍪 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

ShopSphere uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience:

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how you use our website
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Marketing Cookies: Show you relevant advertisements (with consent)

Your Cookie Choices

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner. However, disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

🤝 Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party service providers to operate our platform:

Payment Processors

Secure payment gateways for processing transactions

Shipping Partners

Reliable delivery services for order fulfillment

Analytics Providers

Tools to understand and improve user experience

Marketing Platforms

Email and advertising services (with consent)

All third-party partners are carefully vetted and contractually obligated to protect your data in accordance with this privacy policy.

💳 Payment Processing

Payment security is our top priority at ShopSphere:

Security Measures

  • PCI DSS compliant payment processing
  • SSL encryption for all payment transactions
  • Tokenization of sensitive payment data
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments

Important Note

ShopSphere does not store complete credit card numbers. Payment information is processed by secure third-party payment processors who are PCI DSS compliant.

🔒 Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

Security Technologies

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest
  • Access Controls: Strict authentication and authorization systems
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security assessments and penetration testing
  • Employee Training: Regular security awareness training for our team

While we take reasonable precautions to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
