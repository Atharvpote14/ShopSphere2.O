What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help us remember your preferences, improve your experience, and provide personalized content. ShopSphere uses cookies responsibly to enhance functionality and security.

Frequently Asked Questions

You can manage cookie preferences through our cookie consent banner, browser settings, or by clearing cookies from your device. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies.

Disabling cookies may limit website functionality, affect your shopping experience, and prevent personalized content. Essential cookies are required for basic site operation.

ShopSphere uses cookies responsibly and follows privacy regulations. We don't store personal information in cookies without your consent and use secure encryption methods.
